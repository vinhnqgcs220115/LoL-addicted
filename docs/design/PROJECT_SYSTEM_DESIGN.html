01Executive summary

The product is a per-game decision reconstruction. The season layer is a pattern index over the same facts — not a separate statistics system. Everything currently shipped is the season layer, which is why it cannot answer the question the user actually has.

The core unit

Not a metric. A Decision Record: context, options, observed choice, consequence, evidence, and named missing inputs. Every user-facing claim resolves to one.

The honesty rule

A judgement needing an unobservable input never becomes a verdict. It becomes a review target with a timestamp range. This is a feature, not a limitation.

The drill path

season pattern → affected games → game → timeline → decision → evidence. One continuous flow, first-class, not five disconnected pages.

02Product vision

"Given one of my games: what happened, what did I choose, and what should I have weighed differently?"

The competing product is not OP.GG. It is the replay. For a single game a strong player can scrub to 14:00 and read CS and gold faster than any dashboard. Restating what they already knew mid-game has no value.

What the tool does that the replay cannot

Search at scale

825 games × ~29 minutes is roughly 16,000 minutes of footage. The tool finds the twenty minutes worth watching. No human reviews that corpus.

Remove fog, computed

Enemy jungler position at every decision point. The replay contains it too, but only if you manually track it. The tool states it automatically at every death.

Eliminate hypotheses

"You lost lane, and it was not jungle pressure" is worth more than "you lost lane". Narrowing the explanation is the product.

Detect repetition

One bad minute is noise. The same shape across forty games is a pattern, and no one holds forty games in their head.

Measure change

After changing something you cannot feel whether it worked — thirty games of variance swamps it. The tool measures it.

Ask every question, every game

The same fifteen decision checks run on every game. A human asks whichever ones they happen to think of that day.

The division of labour. The tool searches, locates, and supplies facts. The player diagnoses mechanics. This is not a workaround for weak data — even with perfect telemetry the machine should do the searching and the human should watch the moment.

Non-goals

Live or in-game featuresWin probability predictionGeneric stat trackingMulti-user or public profilesAn LLM layer over unreliable analyticsAll-role analytics

03User workflows

Two modes. Every navigation decision serves one of them.

The evidence panel is the convergence point of the entire product. Both workflows terminate in "why does the system believe this, and what does it not know". Everything else is navigation.

Ordered by what the player needs first. Statistics are context, never the headline.

Lane phase — what survives and what does not

Keep

CS diff, gold diff, XP diff and level diff versus the actual lane opponent, per minute. Level-up timings from LEVEL_UP. Item completion from ITEM_PURCHASED. Per-minute champion-damage exchange. Deaths with full damage attribution.

All measured at 100% presence, corpus-wide.

Cut as clutter

Raw CS without a comparison. CS/min shown alone. Vision score. Damage share. Anything self-referential — comparing the player to their own season average rather than to the opponent in front of them.

A number with no counterparty answers no question.

Wave state must never be implied. CS rate, gold rate and tower-relative position are lane activity proxies. A 14-CS minute may be a stacked crash, two waves under tower, or a jungle camp — the proxy cannot separate them. Where a lane-activity proxy is shown it is labelled as one.

05Per-season final contract

"What kind of mid-laner am I, and what should I work on?" — not "what are my averages?"

Season analysis is an index over per-game decision records. It aggregates the same facts the game view shows, so the two can never disagree. It is built after the per-game engine, not beside it.

BlockQuestionDesign constraint
Current formHow am I doing right now?Recent window plus trend direction. Not a lifetime average.
Recurring patternsWhat keeps happening?Requires repeated evidence. One unusual game is never a pattern. Links to the affected game list.
Champion poolWhat actually works for me?Never a raw win-rate sort — see methodology below.
MatchupsWhich lanes are genuinely hard?Verdict only where the sample supports it; direction with explicit uncertainty otherwise.
ImprovementIs anything changing?A small fixed set of trends, each tied to a named pattern.

Champion evaluation methodology PROPOSED

Ranking is a two-gate process, not a score.

  1. Eligibility gate. A champion is rankable only if an exact one-sided binomial test against the player's own baseline can distinguish its record from that baseline. Below that it is reported as Uncertain with its game count, never ranked.
  2. Ordering among eligible champions uses win rate against baseline, with lane differential at minute 14 shown alongside as corroboration.

This preserves the existing rule that 2 games, 100% never outranks 40 games, 62% — the two-game champion is not ranked lower, it is not ranked at all.

Multiple-comparison correction is required and currently absent. Testing 39 champions at p < 0.05 produces roughly two false verdicts by chance. On the present data the only champion clearing the uncorrected threshold is Zoe (124 games, 58.9%, p = 0.032), which does not survive a Bonferroni correction. Either apply a correction or label uncorrected results as leads. REQUIRES DOMAIN REVIEW

06Decision analysis model

One record shape for every decision, buildable or not. The shape is what makes uncertainty expressible.

DecisionRecord
  decision_id          stable identifier
  match_id, t_start_ms, t_end_ms
  catalogue_entry      FK to the decision catalogue
  category             lane | movement | combat | objective | information | resource
  context              observable state at t_start
  options              plausible alternatives (domain-defined)
  observed_choice      what the player did
  consequence          observable outcome
  cost                 what was given up
  evaluation           good | bad | tradeoff | not_justified
  confidence           high | medium | low
  availability         BUILDABLE | PARTIAL | REPLAY_MANUAL_ONLY | NOT_OBSERVABLE
  missing_inputs[]     telemetry | information_state | domain_rule
  evidence[]           facts with provenance, each traceable to a canonical row
  rule_version         which analysis rule produced this

The availability model

BUILDABLE

Historical Match-V5 supports detection and evaluation. Ships as an automatic verdict.

PARTIAL

Action detectable; a load-bearing judgement input missing. Ships only with the missing half named in the output.

REPLAY_MANUAL_ONLY

No structured source; a human can see it in a replay. Ships as a review target with a timestamp range. Patch-locked.

NOT_OBSERVABLE

No defensible source, replay included. Does not ship at all.

The missing-input model PROPOSED

A single NOT OBSERVABLE label conceals three different problems with three different fixes. This is the highest-value refinement in the document.

ClassMeaningFixExample
telemetry_missingThe entity is absent from all Riot sources.Replay annotation, or never.#6 Missed Crash — wave state
information_state_missingWhat the player could see. Absent from telemetry and not recoverable from replay, since replays play without fog of war.None. Reword the claim.#19 Blind Follow — vision
domain_rule_missingThe data exists. A threshold is undefined.User defines it. No new data.#13 No-Roam — "high value"
Six entries are blocked only by a missing domain rule — #13, #14, #15, #25, #38, #43. They need no telemetry whatsoever, only a number the player decides. These are the cheapest wins in the catalogue and were invisible under the old single label.

Output when judgement is not justified

08:42  candidate decision point
  observed   enemy jungler 618u away · pushed into enemy tower range
             CS +6 this minute · no ally within 3000u
  missing    wave state (telemetry) · vision (information state)
  verdict    not justified
  review     08:42 – 09:05

This is honest, useful, and shippable today. It is also the natural hand-off into replay review.

07Evidence & uncertainty model

Four epistemic levels, never collapsed into one confident sentence.

The UI must make these distinguishable without four labels on every sentence. Resolution: visual weight carries the level. Observed facts are plain text; interpretation is italic and prefixed; unknowns sit in a bordered strip at the card's foot. The evidence panel spells the levels out explicitly on demand.

The evidence panel

Likely the single most trust-building feature. Every automatic claim answers "why did the system say this?" in one click, listing each supporting fact with its canonical source row — and the missing inputs beside them, with equal prominence.

K-Means clustering — classification

DEPRECATED  Remove src/models.py, models/cluster_centroids.json, cluster_labels, and the Patterns tab that renders them.

QuestionFinding
Answers a user question?No. "Which of four behaviour groups is this game" is not a question the player has.
Labels stable?No. IDs permute on essentially every data refresh; the centroid guard fires every time by construction.
Feature set meaningful?No. Three of nine features correlate pairwise at r = 0.75–0.82, making the model close to one-dimensional on death count.
Trustworthy?No. Silhouette 0.193. Across three independent feature sets and k = 2…8 the clusters recover game outcome, not behaviour.
Explanatory in a dashboard?No. A numeric cluster ID cannot be traced to evidence, which the decision model requires of every claim.

Replacement: explicit named patterns computed from decision records — checkable, nameable, stable across refreshes, and carrying no ID binding. Retiring clustering also dissolves the long-standing cluster-ID permutation issue rather than answering it.

09Replay & manual annotation

A separate evidence source with its own provenance. Never Riot telemetry, never silently merged with it.

Replay review is perishable and cannot backfill. Riot replays are patch-locked and will not launch on a later client. Only current-patch games are reviewable — the corpus spanning 15.18 to 16.17 is permanently beyond annotation. This makes annotation a rolling opportunity on recent games, not a historical project.

Lifecycle

decision record with availability = REPLAY_MANUAL_ONLY
        ↓  system emits review candidate (t_start, t_end, what to look for)
review queue, ordered by decision impact, current-patch games only
        ↓  human opens replay at timestamp, observes, records
annotation row  →  linked to match_id + decision_id
        ↓
decision record re-evaluated with annotation as an evidence source

Annotation record PROPOSED

annotations
  annotation_id, match_id, decision_id (nullable)
  t_start_ms, t_end_ms
  kind            wave_state | trade_context | mechanics | note
  payload         JSON — e.g. {state:"slow_push", side:"blue", stacked_waves:2}
  reviewer_confidence   high | medium | low
  game_version    patch at observation time
  created_at
  source          'MANUAL_ANNOTATION'   always; never 'riot'

Annotations are append-only and permanent. They record what a human saw at a point in time and are not revised when rules change — a corrected observation is a new row. DEFER building the capture UI; the schema slot and the review-candidate emission are what must exist first.

Every visualization declares a question, a source, an interpretation, and a next action. If it has no answerable question it is cut.

The first three render a deprecated model. The next two are reference material occupying headline space. The scatter shows a correlation the player cannot act on.

Chart labelling standard. Title states the subject, not the column name — "Your CS versus the opposing mid, by minute", not "CS Difference". A one-line "what this shows" sits beneath. "Why it matters" and "what this cannot conclude" live behind progressive disclosure, so caveats are always available and never in the way.

14Data architecture

Six layers. The boundary rules are what keep interpretation out of the parser and facts out of the UI.

LayerPurposeAllowedProhibitedStorage
RAWImmutable evidenceAppend new files onlyAny modification after savedata/raw/ JSON
CANONICALWhat happenedParse, normalize, type, keyAny domain interpretation, any thresholdDuckDB
DERIVEDComputed valuesArithmetic and algorithms with documented methodJudgement, naming a behaviourDuckDB + provenance
DECISIONCandidate decision pointsRule application producing recordsPresentation concernsDuckDB + rule_version
ANALYSISUser-facing conclusionsInterpretation, recommendation, aggregationReading data/raw/ directlyViews / functions
PRESENTATIONRenderingLayout, formatting, cachingAny business logic whatsoeverdashboard/
Domain knowledge enters at the DECISION layer only. It must never be embedded invisibly inside parsers. A parser that "knows" 14:00 ends laning has smuggled domain knowledge into canonical data, where it cannot be versioned or reviewed. Canonical parsing knows about JSON; it does not know about League.

15Database design

Participant-long canonical core. The current two-participant shape with bolted-on opp_* columns is the root blocker for external-pressure analysis.

Design principles applied

QuestionAnswer
What is immutable?data/raw/ and annotations. Nothing else.
What is recomputable?Everything from CANONICAL downward. A full rebuild from raw must be a routine operation, not an event.
Canonical timeline identity(match_id, frame_index, participant_id) plus timestamp_ms. "Minute" is an interpretation, not an identity — derive it at query time. This survives a future dataset with a different frame interval.
What needs provenance?Every DERIVED and DECISION value: availability_class, derivation_method, confidence.
IdentifiersNo puuid, summonerName or summonerId for any participant. is_subject is sufficient; the subject's own PUUID stays in .env. Storing nine other people's account identifiers is a change in kind from the current database.
Sentinel handlingparticipantId = 0 means "no participant" and appears on t=0 events. It must never join to a player slot.
Retain the full event payload in events.detail even where structured extraction is incomplete. The alternative is discovering in six months that a field is needed from an API whose window on old matches may have closed. Roughly 1.1k events per match is trivial for DuckDB at any plausible corpus size.

16End-to-end data flow

Batch and deterministic from collector through decision engine — the same raw corpus and the same rule version always produce identical decision records. Human-in-the-loop only in the purple block, and its output is a distinct evidence source that never masquerades as telemetry.

18Provenance & versioning

A conclusion such as "this was the correct item" may hold on one patch and not the next. Patch-sensitive conclusions and timeless principles must never mix.

Versioned thingWhere it livesWhy it matters
game_version / patchmatches rowParsed from gameVersion, first two segments. Never hardcode a current patch.
SeasonDerived from patchBoundary is a patch date, not a calendar date.
rule_versiondecisions rowA decision produced by rule v0.1 is not comparable to one from v0.2. Without this, trends silently measure rule changes.
catalogue_versiondecisions rowEntries get renamed, merged and split. Historical records must resolve to the definition in force when written.
domain_versiondocs/domain/ + decision rowDomain constants are patch-sensitive and carry verification dates. Objective spawn timers are an exception — they are derivable from the corpus (earliest observed kill bounds spawn from above: Grubs 8:04, Herald 15:14, Baron 20:15, Dragon 5:24, Atakhan 20:14 across 825 games), so they become measured facts rather than maintained constants. Pooled across patches; compute per patch where patch-accuracy matters.
availability_classEvery derived valueFrom DATA_AUDIT.md, regenerated as the corpus grows.
Corpus-size independence is a standing rule. No count is an architectural assumption. The pipeline enumerates data/raw/ at run time — never from a module constant, a context document, or a design file. A count recorded anywhere is a dated observation, not an input.

19Privacy & publication boundary

Publication is an explicit transformation, not a file copy. It is the only place private data becomes public.

Rules the boundary enforces

  • Explicit allow-list, never a copy. The publisher names each table and column it emits, so a new private column cannot leak by default.
  • No PUUID, ever — and under the new schema no participant identifiers exist to leak in the first place.
  • Timestamps truncated to midnight UTC. Exact kickoff time plus patch, both mid champions and a death sequence was enough to locate a game on third-party sites. All consumers use >= comparisons, so season, patch and date filtering are unaffected.
  • Re-identification review is part of publishing. Per-death positions plus killer champion form an ordered (minute, champion) sequence — a materially stronger fingerprint than champion plus date. Adding a per-game public view requires re-running this assessment. REQUIRES DOMAIN REVIEW
  • Annotations are private by default. Free-text reviewer notes are not published.
Open decision. The per-game view is the centre of the product, and a public dated game list is the most identifying artefact the project would publish. Options: keep Game Review local-only and publish season aggregates; publish with coarsened identifiers; or accept the risk explicitly. This is a user decision and is not resolved here.

21Testing strategy

Tests protect analytical correctness, not coverage percentages. A green suite proves the software works; it does not prove the analysis describes the game.

LayerWhat is testedMethod
RAWCorpus integrity — pairing, duplicates, malformed filesThe audit script, re-runnable
CANONICALParser correctness; every participant maps to the right slotFixtures + the cross-payload invariant over the full corpus
DERIVEDFormula correctness and invariantsUnit tests; edge cases — zero deaths, missing minute, remake with blank teamPosition, participantId = 0
DECISIONRules fire when they should and stay silent when they should notSynthetic scenarios plus hand-labelled real matches. The most important tests in the project
ANALYSISNo claim exceeds its evidence; PARTIAL always names its missing halfAssertions over emitted records
DASHBOARDRenders without exception; drill path reachableStreamlit AppTest smoke tests
PUBLICATIONNo identifiers, no precise timestampsAssertions on the built deploy DB
The two validation layers are independent. Software correctness is tests. Analytical correctness is a human reading a generated report against a replay or a reference source and marking each line correct, wrong, or unverifiable. Only the second can catch a parser that is 100% green and describing the wrong game.

24Definition of done

The end-to-end experience.

The player opens the dashboard after a session. The Overview says which way current form is moving and names two or three patterns the evidence actually supports — for instance that in losses the champion-damage exchange turns against them around minute three while their CS stays ahead.

They open that pattern. It lists the games it was detected in, with frequency and trend. They pick a recent one.

Game Review opens on a verdict line and the lane-phase curves against the actual opponent. The timeline shows when the choices occurred. They open a flagged decision at 12:01 and read the record: context, the options that existed, what they did, what followed — and that the enemy jungler was 618 units away, with a note that whether it was visible is unknown.

They click through to the evidence panel and see every supporting fact traced to a canonical row, beside the inputs the system does not have.

Two review targets sit at the foot of the page. The game is on the current patch, so they open the replay at 04:00, watch the wave they could not otherwise assess, and record an annotation. The decision re-evaluates with that evidence attached.

Weeks later they return to the same pattern and see whether it still occurs at the same rate.
